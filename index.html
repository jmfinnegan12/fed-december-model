Background

The federal funds target range is 3.75-4.00%. The FOMC meets Dec 8-9 and announces its decision at 2:00 pm ET on Dec 9, with new projections.1,5

  • Chair Kevin Warsh's standard for action: "We must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective, clearly and at sufficient speed." In September the Committee judged it not met and hiked.2
  • The September projections' median put the end-2026 rate one 25bp hike above today's range.3
  • October core CPI rose 0.3%, as in August and September.4 Unemployment was 4.1%.6
  • Waller supports a 25bp hike in December and said 50bp "should be considered" if core CPI ran around 0.3% or higher.7 Logan wants "at least two more" 25bp hikes.8

The question
